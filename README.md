☕ Breva Cafe — Specialty Coffee Website

📌 Overview

Breva Cafe is a landing page for a specialty coffee shop. The website features a hero banner, a dynamic-ready menu section displaying specialty drinks and fresh bakeries, an "Our Story" brand highlight with key achievements, and location/contact details.

"Where Every Cup Tells a Story — Sourced from the world's finest farms, roasted with care, brewed with precision."

🛠️ Tech Stack

HTML5 — Semantic markup for structure and content layout.

CSS3 (style.css) — Custom styling, layout design, and responsive elements.

JavaScript (script.js) — Client-side interaction handling (menu navigation, smooth scroll, etc.).

💡 Key Sections & Features

☕ Hero Banner: Welcome section with call-to-action buttons (SEE OUR MENU, OUR STORY).

🥐 Interactive Menu Grid: Categorized display for Drinks (Espresso, Latte, Matcha, etc.) and Bakeries (Cake, Cheesecake, Éclair, Biscuits).

📖 Our Story & Impact Stats: Highlights company history since 2019 along with key metric cards (12+ Origins, 40k+ Happy Cups, 5 Years in Business, 5★ Rated).

📍 Footer Information: Store hours, physical address (42 Maple Street, NY), and direct contact options.
